    NOTES¶

    fluecy & coherence：

    不要赘述，不要流水账

    linking words

    hesitation-语速很慢，很拖拉

    Grammer：

    要有从句，时态要正确

    Lextical Resouorce：

    情绪，生动的语言

    不要unfamiliar words（抽象的情绪情感的表达）

    可以不按照四个问题的角度答

    第一句话：建立context，包含题目中的所有问题

    • This topic reminds me of ... + 更多信息（个人感受，时间地点）
    • For this question, I would like to talk about … + 更多信息

    College Entrance Examination

    第二句话：

    丰富内容：

    • personal feeling（情绪，个人看法）贯彻始终
    • 说说过去，说说未来【I still haven't gone back to play it even to this day unfortunately】
    • 不要随便替换成YOU，要和自己有关
    • show don't tell
      • 【我很伤心——>我哭了，我本以为会……】
      • 讲个具体的事例

    LINKING WORDS:

    • as for + 题目
    • in terms of
    • when it comes to ...
    • apart from that ...
    - **Last August** 是最简洁、最常用的表达。

- **In August of last year** 更正式，适合书面语。

- **August last year** 更口语化，也很自然。

- **During August of the previous year** 更正式，适合正式场合。

- a tech-related gathering

- She is a senior in the computer science department.

    Describe an occasion when you lost something in a public place.

    • What you lost
    • When and where you lost this thing
    • What you did to try and find it
    • And explain how you felt about losing this thing in a public place

    I remember one time when I lost my keys at a shopping mall. It was a busy Saturday afternoon, and I had just finished shopping. When I got home, I realized my keys were missing. I was really worried because my keys had my dormitory and cabinets keys on them.

    I retraced my steps and went back to the mall. I checked the places I had been, like the store I was shopping in and the coffee shop I had stopped at. I even asked the staff if they had found any keys, but unfortunately, no one had turned them in. I felt really frustrated and anxious, thinking about how I was going to get home and into my house.

    In the end, I had to call my mom to get the door opened and then went to a hardware store to get new keys made. This experience taught me to be more careful with my belongings, especially in crowded places. I also started using a keychain with a tracker, so I don't lose them again.

One of my group members was sick and couldn't come to campus, but we still needed to discuss our progress and allocate the tasks.

    I used my phone to set up a video call with the whole group. We were able to talk through our plans, assign tasks, and make sure everyone was on the same page. It was important because it kept our project on track, even though one of us couldn't be there in person.

    As a result, we finished the project on time and got a good grade. My phone helped us stay connected and work together smoothly, even when we couldn't meet face-to-face.
